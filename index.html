My Garden

There are flowers that bloom once and are gone, and flowers that return every spring without being asked. You are the second kind. You bloom in me every single day without needing a reason, without needing a season. I am better for knowing you. I am softer for loving you. And every flower I will ever see will remind me of something you once made me feel.
